# Woff editorial guidance

Moved from the internal `/blog/tips` page on October 4, 2026. Internal publishing guidance belongs in repository documentation, not the public sitemap.

Preserve the existing 19 article URLs and the current blog design. Review existing articles for accurate ownership, invitations, private notes, limits, retention, and export instructions before adding content. Record actual revisions with `updatedDate`; keep original publication dates.

Three existing guides should offer exact reusable examples: sharing notes without login, moving text between devices, and sharing code with context. Three focused additions cover website handoff, design-package delivery, and temporary sharing before expiry. Avoid promising upload-only collection, password sharing, multi-user document editing, AI tools, or team features that have not shipped.

Useful content pillars:

- Quick sharing: notes, links, files, screenshots, and snippets, with their actual boundaries.
- Client handoffs: included files, the requested decision, next action, and what to download.
- Keeping a copy: export and expiry, with durable records in the user's own archive.

Each guide should solve a concrete task, show an example, describe the relevant limits, link to help or another useful guide, and end with a matching working action. Comparison headlines require real, sourced comparisons; otherwise describe the decision criteria in the title. Covers and screenshots must not imply unavailable controls or fabricated usage statistics.

Keep published and scheduled article selection consistent across index, sitemap, article routes, and related links. New pricing/account pages must describe the pilot's current availability rather than advertise a checkout that is not enabled.
